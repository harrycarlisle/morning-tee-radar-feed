# Reusable Intro Patterns

These are patterns, not current-news claims.

## The Small Detail Lead

Good Morning,

The easy story is [headline]. The more useful story is the small detail underneath it: [specific detail]. That detail tells us more about [player/course/strategy] than the final score alone.

Today we're looking at:
- Why [detail] mattered
- What regular golfers can learn from it
- One thing to watch next

## The Course-Fit Lead

Good Morning,

Some courses identify the best player. Others identify the player who makes the fewest uncomfortable decisions. This week looks like the second kind.

Today we're looking at:
- The shot this course keeps asking for
- The player profile that fits
- One mistake amateurs can avoid

## The Rules-Clarity Lead

Good Morning,

Golf rulings often look strange until you know which part of the rulebook is doing the work. This one is less about vibes and more about procedure.

Today we're looking at:
- What actually happened
- Why the ruling made sense
- The simple version to remember in your own round

## The Normal-Golfer Lesson Lead

Good Morning,

The lesson is not "hit it like a tour player." Helpful, yes. Also not available at checkout. The real lesson is [practical takeaway].

Today we're looking at:
- The tour example
- The regular-golfer version
- A simple way to try it
