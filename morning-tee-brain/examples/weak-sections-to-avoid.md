# Weak Sections To Avoid

## Generic Momentum Copy

Weak:
"After a strong performance, [Player] will look to build momentum heading into next week."

Why weak: it says nothing specific, has no golf lesson, and could apply to anyone.

Better:
"The useful part of [Player]'s week was not the finish. It was how often he left himself uphill putts after missing the green."

## Empty Course Preview

Weak:
"This course will test every part of the players' games."

Why weak: every course can be described that way.

Better:
"This course tests patience more than power. The player who accepts 25 feet from the safe side may beat the player who keeps firing at tucked pins."

## Fake Drama

Weak:
"All eyes will be on [Player] as he faces a defining week."

Why weak: it inflates the stakes without proving them.

Better:
"The week is useful because it shows whether [specific skill] travels to a course that asks a different question."

## Overloaded Stat Stack

Weak:
"He ranks 14th in this, 22nd in that, 8th in another stat, and has three top-20s."

Why weak: facts without a takeaway.

Better:
"The numbers point to one thing: his approach play is good enough to contend, but his misses still leave him short-sided too often."
