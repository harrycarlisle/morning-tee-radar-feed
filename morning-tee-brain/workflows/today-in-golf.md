# Today In Golf Workflow

This document guides Today in Golf writing and review. It does not change the Today in Golf automation.

## Blurb Standard

Each blurb should be:
- Current and sourced.
- Useful in one quick read.
- Clear about why the item matters.
- Short enough to scan.
- Free of fake urgency.

## Good Blurb Shape

1. What is happening.
2. Why golfers should care.
3. One specific detail or takeaway.

Example:
"The interesting part of today's setup is the wind direction. If it holds, the closing holes may reward players who can flight wedges instead of launching everything high."

## Review Checklist

- Source checked today.
- No invented tee times, field notes, injuries, or rankings.
- Date-sensitive claims verified.
- Tone is useful, not breathless.
- No automation or schedule changes made from this workflow doc.
