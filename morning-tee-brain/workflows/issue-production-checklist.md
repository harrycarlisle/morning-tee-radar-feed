# Issue Production Checklist

## Research Checklist

- Current sources gathered.
- Primary sources checked where possible.
- Old Morning Tee examples used only for voice/style.
- Candidate stories ranked 1-5.
- Weak/generic stories removed.

## Draft Checklist

- Good Morning intro leads with the angle.
- Preview bullets are specific.
- Each main section has core fact, why it matters, memorable detail, and golfer takeaway.
- Context is short.
- No bloated intro or generic hype.

## Fact-Check Checklist

- Names, dates, rankings, fields, tee times, and scores verified.
- Quotes verified with source.
- Rules/rulings checked against reliable source.
- Historical comparisons checked.
- Uncertain facts flagged.

## Voice Checklist

- Conversational but useful.
- Natural contractions.
- Light wit only where helpful.
- No stiff sportswriting.
- No "only time will tell" or "build momentum."
- One memorable idea per section.

## Beehiiv Formatting Checklist

- Subject and preview text selected.
- Links work.
- Source notes removed or converted into appropriate links before publishing.
- Sponsor read, if any, is clearly separated and useful.
- Legal/subscriber footer areas are not changed by this local draft process.

## Pre-Send Human Approval Checklist

- Human has read the full issue.
- Human has checked sources.
- Human has approved final copy.
- Human manually publishes/schedules in Beehiiv.
- No script or automation sends the newsletter.
