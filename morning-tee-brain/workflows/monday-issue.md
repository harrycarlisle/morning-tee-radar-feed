# Monday Issue Workflow

Monday is for weekend meaning: what happened, what mattered, and what golfers can learn.

1. Review official tournament results, final-round notes, and reliable recaps.
2. Identify 5-8 candidate stories.
3. Score each story with `story-selection-rules.md`.
4. Pick one lead story and 2-3 supporting sections.
5. Write the Good Morning intro around the best angle, not the biggest headline.
6. Add source notes under every factual section.
7. Include one regular-golfer takeaway.
8. Run the production checklist.
9. Human reviews and manually moves final copy into Beehiiv.

Monday issue bias:
- Results with lessons.
- Decisions under pressure.
- Course-fit confirmation.
- What changed from Thursday's expectations.
- What normal golfers can take to their next round.
