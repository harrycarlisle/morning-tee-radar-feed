# Source Rules

Current news must be checked against reliable current sources. Old Morning Tee examples are style examples only, not current facts.

## Approved Source Types

Prefer:
- Official PGA TOUR, DP World Tour, USGA, R&A, Masters, PGA of America, tournament, player, and course sources.
- Official scoring, field, tee time, and rules pages.
- Reputable golf media with bylines and dates.
- Direct press conference transcripts or verified quotes.
- Data providers already used by Morning Tee, with source notes.

Use cautiously:
- Social posts, only when verified and dated.
- Aggregators, only as leads to primary sources.

Avoid:
- Unsourced quote graphics.
- Rumor accounts.
- Old examples treated as current reporting.
- Screenshots without source context.

## Fact-Check Checklist

Before publishing, verify:
- Player names and spellings.
- Dates, tee times, fields, rankings, standings, and purse details.
- Injury, withdrawal, suspension, or eligibility status.
- Quotes and who said them.
- Rules/ruling details and penalties.
- Stats, records, and historical comparisons.

## Citation Expectations

Drafts should include source notes under each section or in the final source list. A source note can be a URL, "needs verification," or "official scoring page checked."

## Uncertain Facts

If uncertain, write:
- `[VERIFY: source needed for claim]`
- `[UNCERTAIN: do not publish until confirmed]`
- `[SOURCE NOTE: based on official scoring page as of YYYY-MM-DD]`

Never hide uncertainty in polished copy.
