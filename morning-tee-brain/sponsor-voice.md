# Sponsor Voice

Sponsor reads should sound useful, clean, and aligned with Morning Tee. They should not feel like a sudden costume change.

## Tone

Use:
- Practical benefit.
- Specific use case.
- Short setup.
- Clear CTA.
- Honest fit with golfers' lives.

Avoid:
- Fake enthusiasm.
- Overpromising.
- Five adjectives in a row.
- Copy that sounds like it was pasted from a brand deck.

## Structure

1. Reader problem or moment.
2. Sponsor solution.
3. Specific benefit.
4. Clear CTA.

## Example CTA Structures

Golf-product sponsor:
"If your practice sessions keep turning into random range balls, [Sponsor] helps you give each bucket a job. Try it here: [link]"

Travel sponsor:
"If your next golf trip is still eight browser tabs and a group chat, [Sponsor] makes the planning part less painful. Start here: [link]"

Training sponsor:
"If you want one thing to work on this week, start with [specific benefit]. [Sponsor] can help: [link]"

## Keep It Morning Tee

Connect the sponsor to the issue when possible, but do not force it. A good sponsor read should feel like a useful aside, not an interruption.
