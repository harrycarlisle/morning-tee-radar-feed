# Recurring Sections

Each section needs one core fact, one why-it-matters point, and one memorable detail.

## Today in Golf

Purpose: quick, useful golf context for the day.

Use when: there is a current event, schedule note, player trend, or useful golf-history angle.

Format: 2-4 tight bullets or one short paragraph.

Example angle: "The field looks normal until you notice how many players rely on high approaches into firm greens."

Example copy: "Today is a course-fit day. The leaderboard may reward players who can flight wedges down, not just players who hit it high and hope."

## Tour Notes

Purpose: fast tour context without turning into a news dump.

Use when: several smaller stories matter but none deserves a full section.

Format: 3 bullets, each with a takeaway.

Example angle: "Three small things that explain the week better than the headline."

## Smart Golfer Tip

Purpose: connect tour observation to a normal-golfer lesson.

Use when: the story reveals strategy, practice, decision-making, or course management.

Format: mistake, better frame, try this.

Example copy: "If you short-side yourself often, the fix may not be a better flop shot. It may be aiming at the fat side of the green more often."

## Rules Corner

Purpose: make rulings understandable without legal fog.

Use when: a ruling, drop, penalty, embedded-ball issue, provisional, or relief situation is relevant.

Format: what happened, rule bucket, simple takeaway.

## Shot Question

Purpose: make readers think like players.

Use when: a shot choice, club choice, layup, aggressive line, or recovery decision matters.

Format: situation, options, smart play, lesson.

## Course Fit

Purpose: explain why a course favors certain players or skills.

Use when: previewing or explaining tournament performance.

Format: course demand, player match, golfer takeaway.

## Player Trend

Purpose: show what is changing in a player's game.

Use when: a player is gaining/losing with a specific skill or making a strategic shift.

Format: trend, evidence, what to watch.

## What We Learned

Purpose: convert a tournament or issue into one clear takeaway.

Use when: wrapping a week or explaining the main lesson.

Format: one sentence lesson, supporting detail, regular-golfer connection.

## One Thing to Watch

Purpose: preview without hype.

Use when: there is a clear variable that could shape an event.

Format: variable, why it matters, what would confirm it.

## Golf Mini / Product CTA

Purpose: mention Morning Tee products in a useful, non-pushy way.

Use when: relevant to the issue topic.

Format: reader problem, product fit, one clear action.
